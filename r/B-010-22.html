The resolution’s text

Resolution No. B-10-22 From: Legislators Christopher A. Robins, Mark J. Grozio and Jeffrey Elder. Dated: December 13, 2022 BUDGET MODIFICATION SALES TAX WHEREAS, the Sales Tax amount appropriated in the 2023 budget is $84,650,000, now, therefore, be it RESOLVED, that sales tax revenue be increased in the 2023 Budget by $2,184,869, and be if further RESOLVED, that the following budget modification be effectuated: INCREASE REVENUE: A.07.1325.000 41110.01   Sales & Use Tax-General   $1,157,981 A.07,1325.000 41110,02   Sales & Use Tax-Medicaid   $1,026,888 $2,184,869 DECREASE REVENUE: A.07.1325.000 41001.00   Real Property Tax   $2,184,869 Moved by Godfrey, seconded by Nemi. Rejected. 3 Ayes - Elder, Grozio Robins, 12 Noes, 0 Absent.
