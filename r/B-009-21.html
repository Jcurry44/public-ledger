The resolution’s text

Resolution No. B-09-21 From: Legislators Dennis F. Virtuoso, Anita Mullane, Owen T. Steed, Mark J. Grozio and Christopher A. Robins. Dated; December 14, 2021 BUDGET MODIFICATION SALES TAX WHEREAS, the Sales Tax amount appropriated in the 2022 budget is $74,680,000, now, therefore be it RESOLVED, that sales tax revenue be increased in the 2022 Budget by $750,000, and be if further RESOLVED, that the following budget modification be effectuated: INCREASE REVENUE: A.07.1325.000 41110.01   Sales & Use Tax-General   375,000 A.07.1325.000 41110.02   Sales & Use Tax-Medicaid   371000 $750,000 DECREASE REVENUE: A.07.1325.000 41001.00   Real Property Tax   $750,000 Moved by Robins, seconded by Grozio. Rejected. 4 Ayes, 10 Noes, 1 Absentm Steed.
