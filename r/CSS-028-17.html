Resolution No. CSS-028-17 From: Community Safety & Security and Administration Committees Dated: May 16, 2017 NIAGARA COUNTY SHERIFF'S OFFICE OFFICE OF VICTIM SERVICES GRANT WHEREAS, the Niagara County Sheriff's Office was notified in writing that the New York State Office of Victim Services has extended the multi-year grant which was for a term of October 1, 2014 to September 30, 2017 into a term from October 1, 2014 to September 30, 2019, and WHEREAS, the grant amount was increased from $265,262 for the 2016-2017 grant fiscal year to $331,577.50 for the 2017-2018 grant fiscal year, and…Cut to fit one sheet.
