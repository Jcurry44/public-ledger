The resolution’s text

Resolution No. CW-001-21 From: Committee of the Whole. Dated: January 5, 2021 REGIONAL AGREEMENT BETWEEN NIAGARA AND ERIE COUNTY TO PERFORM AUTOPSY SERVICES WHEREAS, Niagara County and Erie County entered into an agreement in 2002 that allows the Erie County Medical Examiner's Office to perform all of Niagara County's autopsies, which agreement has subsequently been renewed, and WHEREAS, the current agreement will expire December 31, 2020, and WHEREAS, Erie County has offered us a one-year agreement at $2,100/full autopsy and $l,350/extemal exam, along with other associated fees, and…
