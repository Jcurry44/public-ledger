Resolution of the County Legislature of the County of Niagara, New York (the "County"), (i) authorizing the County to undertake a Lease-Purchase Financing in the principal amount not to exceed $6,250,000 for the equipment and other personal property to be acquired and installed as part of the County's energy performance contract project; (II) approving the award of such financing; (ill) authorizing the execution and delivery of an Equipment Lease/Purchase Agreement with respect thereto and of other documents required in connection therewith; and (IV) authorizing the taking of all Other Actions necessary to the consummation of the transactions contemplated by this resolution

Resolution CW-018-18, adopted 14–0 (1 absent) by the Niagara County Legislature on Sept 18, 2018.
