The resolution’s text

Resolution No. B-08-20 From: Legislators Dennis F. Virtuoso, Anita Mullane, Owen T. Steed, Mark J. Grozio and Christopher A. Robins. Dated: December 8, 2020 BUDGET MODIFICATION EMERGENCY MANAGEMENT BUDGET WHEREAS, the Emergency Management 2021 Budget includes an appropriation for $62,000 for Contractual Expenses, now, therefore, be it RESOLVED, that $62,000 be decreased in the 2021 County Budget as follows: DECREASE REVENUE: A.07.1325.000 41001.00 Real Property Tax $62,000 DECREASE APPROPRIATIONS: A.19.3640.000 74500.01 Contractual Expenses $62,000 Moved by Virtuoso, seconded by Steed. Rejected. 4 Ayes ~ Grozio, Robins, Steed, Virtuoso, 8 Noes, 3 Absent - Godfrey, Hill, Mullane.
