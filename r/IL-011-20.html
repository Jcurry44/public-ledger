Resolution No. IL-011-20 From: Legislator John Syracuse. Dated: March 10, 2020 SUPPORT FOR COUNTY FUNDING TO HELP WITH LOCAL MATCH FOR OLCOTT REDI PROJECTS WHEREAS, lakeshore towns in Niagara County have been faced with unprecedented damage due to Lake Ontario flooding in 2017 and 2019, and WHEREAS, the Town of Newfane is working will all levels of government to find ways to mitigate damage from future flooding allowing for economic recovery, growth, and development, including tourism, and…
