The resolution’s text

Resolution No. B-10-21 From: Legislators Dennis F. Virtuoso, Anita Mullane, Owen T. Steed, Mark J. Grozio and Christopher A. Robins. Dated: December 14, 2021 BUDGET MODIFICATION DEBT RESERVE WHEREAS, the Debt Reserve amount appropriated in the 2022 budget is $765,000, now, therefore be it RESOLVED, that the debt reserve amount be increased in the 2022 budget by $300,000, and be if further RESOLVED, that the following budget modification be effectuated: INCREASE REVENUE: A.07.9901.000 45031.20 Debt Reserve $300,000 DECREASE REVENUE: A.07.1325.000 41001.00 Real Property Tax $300,000 Moved by Robins, seconded by Mullane.
