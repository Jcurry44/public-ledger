WHEREAS, due to the noncompliance of Local Law IL-041-21 with the October 27, 2021 amendment to sections 34 and 10 of the New York State Home Rule Law it is necessary to amend Local Law IL-041-21 prior to filing it with the Secretary of State's Office, and WHEREAS, it is recommended by the Niagara County Attorney that the deviation rate of the fifteen (15) election districts be amended prior to any Reapportionment Plan being filed with the New York State Secretary of State, now, therefore, be it…Cut to fit one sheet.
