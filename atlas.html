What was kept
Only the file’s revenue and expenditure rows are counted. Its balance-sheet rows are left out, and so are, before 2013, its separate “Other Sources” and “Other Uses” segments (from 2013 they sit inside revenue and expenditure and are counted).

How this page was built

Method. Parsed from the NYS Comptroller’s statewide files for cities, towns and villages, joined on county, flows only. These are self-reported annual financial reports, on the same method as every other page of Public Ledger: as filed, reviewed by the state but not audited, no year filled in, every missing year named. Populations are the 2020 Census via New York State’s official mirror (data.ny.gov sxhg-qquj). Each row’s and card’s figures are that government’s latest filing; the small lines span 1995–2026. Growth is measured inside the current reporting basis (2013 onward) only. Per-resident figures are context, not a scorecard: a government’s own services, a single 2020 population and different latest filing years are not like-for-like. Boundaries: U.S. Census Bureau TIGERweb (tigerWMS_Current): County Subdivisions layer 22, Incorporated Places layer 28, retrieved Oct 1, 2026, generalised for a county map.
