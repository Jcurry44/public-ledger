Method and sources

Method. Parsed from the NYS Comptroller’s statewide class files (cities, towns, villages), joined on county, flows only. Self-reported AFR data, on the same method as every other page of Public Ledger: as filed, desk-reviewed but not audited, no interpolation, every missing year named. Populations are the 2020 Census via New York State’s official mirror (data.ny.gov sxhg-qquj). Each card’s figures are that government’s latest filing; sparklines span 1995–2025 at each government’s own scale unless you choose one scale per class. Growth is measured inside the current reporting basis (2013 onward) only. Per-resident figures are context, not a scorecard: a government’s own services, a single 2020 population and different latest filing years are not like-for-like. Boundaries: U.S. Census Bureau TIGERweb (tigerWMS_Current): County Subdivisions layer 22, Incorporated Places layer 28, retrieved 2026-10-01, generalised for a county map.

Companions: the City of North Tonawanda edition (reconciled warrant register, briefs, exception report) — its card above opens it, and its filings in the county are one page over · the County Edition (drill-downs, budget vs actual, the shared sales tax) · all twenty ledgers as a list.
