Method

Where every figure comes from

✓
Checked: every figure is the government’s own filing, as filed20 governments + the county · 112,537 line-item rows read · no filing year missing inside a span2 of 20 have a year past its filing deadline that is not yet in the Comptroller data, as checked Nov 9, 2026 (named in their rows)

Parsed from the NYS Comptroller’s statewide files for cities, towns and villages, joined on county, flows only. These are self-reported annual financial reports, on the same method as every other page of Public Ledger: as filed, reviewed by the state but not audited, no year filled in, every missing year named. Populations are the 2020 Census via New York State’s official mirror (data.ny.gov sxhg-qquj). Each row’s and card’s figures are that government’s latest filing; the small lines span 1995–2026. Growth is measured inside the current reporting basis (2013 onward) only. Per-resident figures are context, not a scorecard: a government’s own services, a single 2020 population and different latest filing years are not like-for-like. Boundaries: U.S. Census Bureau TIGERweb (tigerWMS_Current): County Subdivisions layer 22, Incorporated Places layer 28, retrieved Oct 1, 2026, generalised for a county map.

Source. NYS Office of the State Comptroller (OSC), Financial Data for Local Governments, Detailed Account-Level Data: the files city_all_years.zip, town_all_years.zip, village_all_years.zip and county_all_years.zip; snapshot date Oct 30, 2026 for 2013–2025 and Dec 29, 2023 for 1995–2012 in each file. Latest filings: 2025 or newer for 18 governments; 2024 for North Tonawanda and Royalton (files downloaded Nov 9, 2026). The county’s own card and its sales-tax line are read from the county file; each government’s own ledger gives its entity code and the exact rows it sums.

What was kept
Only the file’s revenue and expenditure rows are counted. Its balance-sheet rows are left out, and so are, before 2013, its separate “Other Sources” and “Other Uses” segments (from 2013 they sit inside revenue and expenditure and are counted).
