How to read these numbers

Counts come from the decisions register — 5,871 resolutions parsed from the county’s published agendas and minutes, with 95% of votes matched where minutes are machine-readable. “Moved” and “seconded” reflect the chamber’s practice of one member moving the consent calendar, which is why the floor leader’s count towers. Attendance is the share of machine-readable meetings, within the member’s own first-to-last active years, where the minutes do not record them absent — scanned or unposted minutes can’t count against anyone. Names are canonicalized against the roster (OCR variants folded in, every fix logged at build time). No party, district, or biography here — only what the record shows.
