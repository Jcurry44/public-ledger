How to read these numbers

Counts come from the decisions register — 5,953 resolutions parsed from the county’s published agendas and minutes, with 97% of votes matched where minutes carry a native text layer, plus votes read from scanned minutes by local OCR (each labeled as such in the register, tallies bounded by the chamber’s 15 seats). “Moved” and “seconded” reflect the chamber’s practice of one member moving the consent calendar, which is why the floor leader’s count towers. Attendance is the share of vote-readable meetings, within the member’s own first-to-last active years, where the minutes do not record them absent — scans OCR could not read and unposted minutes can’t count against anyone. Names are canonicalized against the roster (OCR variants folded in, every fix logged at build time). No party, district, or biography here — only what the record shows.
