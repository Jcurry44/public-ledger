The county’s sales tax, shared 2024

Every year the county hands a share of its sales tax to every city, town and village inside it — its filing carries the whole thing as one unlabeled line (A19854 “Distribution of Sales Tax”). The county never names the recipients. Their own filings do. Below, the county’s one line, rebuilt from twenty governments’ books.

The method

Built exactly like the city edition. Parsed from the Comptroller’s statewide county files — 16,772 account-level rows for Niagara County, 1995–2025 — joined on municipal code (never entity name), flows only, balance-sheet rows excluded. The 2013 schema change is resolved by column name per era and marked on every chart. The build is a gate, not a report: if any year goes missing or a total turns implausible, the generator halts and this page does not update.

✓ BUILD GATES PASS: 31 consecutive years · totals in bounds · schema mapping continuous

Self-reported AFR data: OSC desk-reviews these filings but does not audit them. Peer populations are the 2020 Census (P1). Figures as filed; no inflation adjustment.
