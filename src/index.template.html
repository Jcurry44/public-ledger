Schedule 5 · The boundary

What this cannot show

A claims register answers a narrow question. Here is what it cannot answer, and why.

Nothing is aged

The check-date column is populated on well under 1% of rows. There is no days-outstanding, no aging bucket, and no open-item view here.

Duplicate payments can't be detected

A warrant is pre-payment approval, not a cleared-check register. Same-invoice groupings here are split allocations across accounts, not double payments.

Year-over-year is unsafe

2025 and 2026 are both here, but every March and April 2025 warrant was published as an image scan and cannot be read. Comparing the two years would report a fall in spending that is an artifact of the filing cabinet.

Approved, not necessarily paid

These are claims presented for approval. A line appearing here does not establish that a payment cleared, or in what amount.

Purchasing cards are summarised

Card warrants post as one line per charge account, so a card line is a category total, not a single merchant transaction.

It is not a budget

This is disbursement approval only. It carries no appropriation, no encumbrance balance, and no revenue — so it cannot say whether any department is on budget.
