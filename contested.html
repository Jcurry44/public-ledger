A quiz on the record

Each question shows two real resolutions of the Niagara County Legislature from the same year. One had a no vote and one did not. Pick the one you think had a no vote; the answer shows its recorded tally and links the minutes.

Ten questions, dealt at random from 25 pairs in 2021–2026. This is a quiz about the record, not part of it: the register is the record.
