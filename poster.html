Every resolution, one mark each

Each mark is one resolution of the Niagara County Legislature, 2015–2026: 6,105 in all, newest year at the top. Of the 5,787 whose vote was read, 104 had a no vote (1.8%); those stand up from the rest, taller for every no.

How to read the marks

Each year is a row, in the order the county numbered its resolutions within the year (position is sequence, not the calendar). The 104 that had a no vote stand up from the row, taller for every no, with an open ring on top. A solid dot on top means it failed outright: 45 times in 12 years, 15 of them at one meeting (Dec 8, 2015). The other 318 are the faint marks: no vote read yet, so they are not counted either way.

Every Niagara County Legislature resolution 2015 to 2026, newest year at the top, one mark each. Resolutions that had a no vote are tall marks with an open ring on top, and failed ones carry a solid dot on top. A text list of the newest split votes follows.
