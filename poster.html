The Quiet Chamber

Every mark is one resolution, 6,051 in all, newest year at the top, in the order the county numbered them within the year. Of the 5,692 whose vote was read, 98% passed without a single no — the 104 that drew one stand up in the record, taller for every no. The other 359 are the faint marks: no vote read yet. A filled head means it failed outright: 45 times in 12 years, 15 of them at one meeting (December 8, 2015).
