How to read the marks

Each year is a row, in the order the county numbered its resolutions within the year (position is sequence, not the calendar). The 104 that had a no vote stand up in the record, taller for every no. A filled head means it failed outright: 45 times in 12 years, 15 of them at one meeting (Dec 8, 2015). The other 318 are the faint marks: no vote read yet, never counted as quiet.
