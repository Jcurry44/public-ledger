Every resolution, one mark each

Each mark is one resolution of the Niagara County Legislature, 2015–2026: 6,099 in all, newest year at the top. Of the 5,883 whose vote was read, 109 had a no vote (1.9%); those stand up from the rest, taller for every no.

On a phone each year is counted in words, and its marks are only the votes that had a no: tap one to read it, tap again to open it.

How to read the marks

Each year is a row, in the order the county numbered its resolutions within the year (position is sequence, not the calendar). The 109 that had a no vote stand up from the row, taller for every no, with an open ring on top. A solid dot on top means it failed outright: 48 times in 12 years, 15 of them at one meeting (Dec 8, 2015). The other 216 are the faint marks: no vote read yet, so they are not counted either way.

Every Niagara County Legislature resolution 2015 to 2026, newest year at the top, one mark each. Resolutions that had a no vote are tall marks with an open ring on top, and failed ones carry a solid dot on top. A text list of the newest split votes follows.

5,774 of the 5,883 votes read had no one voting no · 109 had a no vote (1.9%) · 48 failed outright · 12 years. The faint, short marks are the 216 resolutions whose vote the county’s minutes have not yielded; they are not counted either way.

Split votes, newest first

Titles are the county’s own wording, from its agendas or its minutes (one printed in capitals keeps the capitals the county gives its names elsewhere). Where the printing ran two words together, the space is put back and the record shows what was printed. Every split vote in 2024–2026; each opens its record.
