Check the books

Corrections

Every figure here is read from the city’s and the county’s own documents and checked against the totals they print. When something is still wrong, it is fixed where the mistake starts and listed below with the date.

Report a correction

It opens an email with the page you came from (and, on a receipt, the warrant, PO, line and page). Say what is wrong and, if you can, where the right figure is printed.

How a correction is made

Each report is checked against the document the figure came from. If the site is wrong, the cause is fixed — the reading of the document, the arithmetic or the label — every page is rebuilt from it, and the correction is added to the list below. If the city’s or the county’s own document is wrong, the site keeps the figure as printed and says so beside it. The site is independent: it is not produced by or for the City of North Tonawanda or Niagara County.

Every correction so far (5)

  1. Oct 30, 2026Tax estimate (the nine cities compared)

    What was wrong. Lockport, Dunkirk, Olean and Batavia were shown at their surrounding town's tax rate, not the city's: Lockport's city line read 4.24 per $1,000 for FY2025 when the city's published rate is 10.42.

    What changed. Each city is matched by its own state code (SWIS), and the build stops if a city's rate does not match the state's published rate for that year.

  2. Oct 30, 2026Warrant dates (council briefs, the register, the feed)

    What was wrong. Seven warrants were dated by the day their report was printed rather than the council meeting that approved them; the brief for the Aug 19, 2025 meeting said Aug 15, 2025.

    What changed. Every warrant is dated by the meeting in the city's own file name, with the printed date shown beside it where it differs.

  3. Oct 30, 2026The front page's headline

    What was wrong. On a night with two warrants the headline gave the larger one only: "$1.59 million" for Oct 20, 2026, when the council approved $1,641,618 that night.

    What changed. The headline, the Past meetings card, each council brief and the feed state one total for the whole meeting.

  4. Oct 30, 2026The front page's "Where it went" bars

    What was wrong. A bar was labelled "Water, sewer & golf" on a night with no golf-course lines in it.

    What changed. The bar names only the funds it holds that night ("Water & sewer").

  5. Oct 30, 2026Exception report

    What was wrong. A city employee's petty-cash account was listed by name among vendors that might belong on a contract.

    What changed. Petty-cash accounts are not vendors and are left out of that flag; the names of private individuals the city pays are withheld across the site (each line keeps its amount, account and page).
