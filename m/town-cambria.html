Town of Cambria’s money

The Town of Cambria took in $6.9M in 2025. It spent $6.7M, 9.8% more than the year before. 31 years of the town’s own annual reports to the state, back to 1995.

Towns & cities: all twenty, on the map and ranked →

What these numbers are

Self-reported by the town to the NYS Comptroller, which reviews the filings at a desk but does not audit them. Figures are as filed, not adjusted for inflation. The town does not publish an itemized claims register on its own site: board minutes give fund totals for the vouchers approved, never payees (checked Oct 5, 2026, source).

Method

Method: where these numbers come from

The Comptroller reviews these filings at a desk but does not audit them. Figures as filed; no inflation adjustment. Populations are the 2020 Census, via New York State’s official mirror (data.ny.gov sxhg-qquj, retrieved July 31, 2026).

Source. NYS Office of the State Comptroller (OSC), Financial Data for Local Governments, Detailed Account-Level Data: file town_all_years.zip; Town of Cambria, entity code 290311200000; snapshot date Oct 30, 2026 for 2013–2025 and Dec 29, 2023 for 1995–2012. Newest filing: 2025.

What was kept
5,701 rows, 1995–2025. Only the file’s revenue and expenditure rows are counted. Its balance-sheet rows are left out, and so are, before 2013, its separate “Other Sources” and “Other Uses” segments (from 2013 they sit inside revenue and expenditure and are counted).
How it was built

Built the same way as the city ledger. The Comptroller publishes every town’s accounts as one statewide file; this page takes Cambria’s 31 years of account-level rows (5,701 of them), matched by the state’s own code for the town, money in and money out only (balance-sheet rows are left out). The state changed its reporting format in 2013; every chart marks the change. If a year goes missing or a total looks wrong, the page does not update.
