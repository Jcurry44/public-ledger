City of Lockport’s money

The City of Lockport took in $58.4M and spent $57.2M in 2025, 11.5% more spending than 2024. 31 years of the city’s own annual reports to the state, 1995–2025.

What these numbers are

Self-reported by the city to the NYS Comptroller, which reviews the filings at a desk but does not audit them. Figures are as filed, not adjusted for inflation. The city publishes itemized accounts-payable data (payments to vendors, not the claims its council approved) at its open financial data portal (Excel, FY2018 through April 2026), not yet read here.

Method

Method: where these numbers come from

The Comptroller reviews these filings at a desk but does not audit them. Figures as filed; no inflation adjustment. Populations are the 2020 Census, via New York State’s official mirror (data.ny.gov sxhg-qquj, retrieved July 31, 2026).

Source. NYS Office of the State Comptroller (OSC), Financial Data for Local Governments, Detailed Account-Level Data: file city_all_years.zip; City of Lockport, entity code 290227000000; snapshot date Sept 30, 2026 for 2013–2025 and Dec 29, 2023 for 1995–2012. Newest filing: 2025.

What was kept
6,653 rows, 1995–2025. Only the file’s revenue and expenditure rows are counted. Its balance-sheet rows are left out, and so are, before 2013, its separate “Other Sources” and “Other Uses” segments (from 2013 they sit inside revenue and expenditure and are counted).
How it was built

Built the same way as the city ledger. The Comptroller publishes every city’s accounts as one statewide file; this page takes Lockport’s 31 years of account-level rows (6,653 of them), matched by the state’s own code for the city, money in and money out only (balance-sheet rows are left out). The state changed its reporting format in 2013; every chart marks the change. If a year goes missing or a total looks wrong, the page does not update.
