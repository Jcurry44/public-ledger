Where the Town of Wheatfield’s money comes from, and where it goes

31 years of the town’s own annual filings to the state — self-reported, desk-reviewed but not audited by OSC. The town does not publish an itemised claims register on its own site — each bill-payment item gives a voucher range, a grand total and fund subtotals, never payees (checked 10/05/2026, source). This edition is the filings story: complete, and drillable to the account line.

The method

How it was built

Built exactly like every Public Ledger edition. Parsed from the Comptroller’s statewide town files — 6,803 account-level rows for Wheatfield, 1995–2025 — joined on municipal code (never entity name), flows only, balance-sheet rows excluded. The 2013 schema change is resolved by column name per era and marked on every chart. The build is a gate, not a report: if any year goes missing or a total turns implausible, the generator halts and this page does not update.

✓ BUILD GATES PASS: 31 consecutive years · totals in bounds · schema mapping continuous

Self-reported AFR data: OSC desk-reviews these filings but does not audit them. Populations are the 2020 Census, via New York State’s official mirror (data.ny.gov sxhg-qquj, retrieved 2026-07-31). Figures as filed; no inflation adjustment.
