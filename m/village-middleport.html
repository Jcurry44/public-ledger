Village of Middleport’s money

The Village of Middleport took in $2.1M and spent $2.0M in 2026, 3.8% less spending than 2025. 32 years of the village’s own annual reports to the state, 1995–2026.

What these numbers are

Self-reported by the village to the NYS Comptroller, which reviews the filings at a desk but does not audit them. Figures are as filed, not adjusted for inflation. The village does not publish an itemized claims register on its own site: minutes cite an abstract number and fund totals, but the abstract itself is not posted (checked Oct 5, 2026, source).

Method

Method: where these numbers come from

The Comptroller reviews these filings at a desk but does not audit them. Figures as filed; no inflation adjustment. Populations are the 2020 Census, via New York State’s official mirror (data.ny.gov sxhg-qquj, retrieved July 31, 2026).

Source. NYS Office of the State Comptroller (OSC), Financial Data for Local Governments, Detailed Account-Level Data: file village_all_years.zip; Village of Middleport, entity code 290472803080; snapshot date Sept 30, 2026 for 2013–2026 and Dec 29, 2023 for 1995–2012. Newest filing: 2026.

What was kept
4,208 rows, 1995–2026. Only the file’s revenue and expenditure rows are counted. Its balance-sheet rows are left out, and so are, before 2013, its separate “Other Sources” and “Other Uses” segments (from 2013 they sit inside revenue and expenditure and are counted).
How it was built

Built the same way as the city ledger. The Comptroller publishes every village’s accounts as one statewide file; this page takes Middleport’s 32 years of account-level rows (4,208 of them), matched by the state’s own code for the village, money in and money out only (balance-sheet rows are left out). The state changed its reporting format in 2013; every chart marks the change. If a year goes missing or a total looks wrong, the page does not update.
