Where the school district’s money comes from, and where it goes

The district behind the biggest line on a North Tonawanda tax bill — 32 years of its own filings to the state. The school district does not publish an itemised claims register on its own site — Board of Education agendas carry a semi-monthly claims-auditor report, one total per fund and a short exceptions list, not a payee list (checked 10/05/2026, source). This edition is the filings story: complete, and drillable to the account line.

The method

How it was built

Built exactly like every Public Ledger edition. Parsed from the Comptroller’s statewide school-district files — 7,231 account-level rows for this district, FY1995–FY2026 — joined on municipal code (never entity name), flows only, balance-sheet rows excluded. The 2013 schema change is resolved by column name per era and marked on every chart. The build is a gate, not a report: if any year goes missing or a total turns implausible, the generator halts and this page does not update.

✓ BUILD GATES PASS: 32 consecutive years · totals in bounds · schema mapping continuous

Self-reported AFR data: OSC desk-reviews these filings but does not audit them. School fiscal years run July–June; each year label is the June it ends. The $453 of every $1,000 is the school rate over the 2025 published rates ($11.01 of $24.31 per $1,000: county $5.54, city $7.76), before STAR and the library levy. Figures as filed; no inflation adjustment.
