About Public Ledger

Where the money in North Tonawanda and Niagara County comes from, where it goes, and how to check it, built from public records.

Who made it

Built by Joe Curry · Curry Studio, a North Tonawanda resident, who also reads every correction.

Why

The records are public, but they sit in separate PDFs and filings. Here they are in one place, each figure linked to the document it came from, so a resident can check it.

Who pays

No one has paid for any of this site: no sponsor, no donations, and GitHub Pages hosts it for free.

Affiliation

Not affiliated with, endorsed by, or produced for the City of North Tonawanda or Niagara County.

Ads and tracking

No ads, no analytics, no cookies.

How it’s checked

Each City warrant prints its own totals, so nothing here is self-graded. 52 of 52 warrants tie to those totals, through the Oct 20, 2026 warrant.

How it’s checked, warrant by warrant.

What it cannot show
  • Approved, not necessarily paid. The City’s claims are presented for approval. A claim here does not show that a check cleared.
  • Duplicate payments can’t be detected. A warrant is approval before payment, not a register of cleared checks.
  • Private individuals are not named. Refunds, judgments and reimbursements to private people keep their amounts and purposes; their names are withheld.
  • A card claim is a category total. Purchasing-card warrants post one line per charge account, not one per merchant.
  • A county ceiling is not spending. It is the most one resolution allows. Ceilings are never added together here.
